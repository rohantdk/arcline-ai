The problem

The answer is already in your company. Your team just can't find it fast.

A buyer emails at 7 pm. They need the specification sheet from last season's order and a revised quote by morning. The sheet is in someone's inbox. The price list is a spreadsheet only one person understands. That person is travelling.

Every exporter knows this evening. The information exists; it's spread across email, Excel, Tally and shared folders.

Meanwhile, your staff paste price lists and buyer emails into free AI tools to save time. Once that data leaves your company, you can't get it back.
